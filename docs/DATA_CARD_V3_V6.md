# Data card: the v3–v6 datasets

The [v1 data card](DATA_CARD.md) covers the sci-Plex3 A549 well-level benchmark. This card covers the data
the cell-level versions used: sci-Plex3 **per cell** (v3, v4), **OP3** (v4) and **Tahoe-100M** (v5, v6).
All three are public and none was generated by this project. "Untouched" anywhere in this repository means
*this project had not scored it before*, never that it is private or unpublished.

Companion: [model card, v3–v6](MODEL_CARD_V3_V6.md).

## sci-Plex3 per cell — v3 and v4 exploratory

**Source.** The three 24 h RDS files of GEO GSM4150378, SHA256-verified
([source_files.tsv](../manifests/v3/source_files.tsv)). Extraction ran on Kaggle under conda-forge R 4.3.1,
the v2 runtime. `export_context_rds.R` is reused unchanged, and the new MCF7 and K562 outputs are
**byte-identical** to the v2 extraction receipts, including the two gzip count files.
[v3_export_cells.R](../scripts/v3_export_cells.R) adds per-cell counts for the 2,000 frozen genes plus each
cell's all-gene library size; its per-well sums equal the pseudobulk counts exactly in all three cell types,
and no cell has a zero library.

**Contexts.** Twelve: A549, K562 and MCF7 × 10, 100, 1,000 and 10,000 nM, all at 24 h. v3's Q1 uses A549
1 µM alone, with exactly the information the frozen v1 methods had.

**The leakage boundary is physical.** [v3_prepare.py](../scripts/v3_prepare.py) writes a model-cell archive
containing only training-compound, validation-compound and vehicle cells — 454,164 nuclei. The 84,572
test-compound nuclei and the 43,041 nuclei of excluded formulations are dropped **before any model code
runs**, and the GPU kernels mount only that archive. Validation and training truth are computed there; test
truth is not.

**Test outcomes already open.** All twelve contexts had their test outcomes opened across v1–v3, so by v4
sci-Plex3 is exploratory only. v3's confirmatory endpoint used the six contexts never scored before —
A549 and K562 at 10 nM, 100 nM and 10 µM — each passing the count gate with 25–26 eligible test compounds
in 23–24 scaffold groups. MCF7 10 µM fails the gate (18 compounds, 17 groups) and is descriptive, as in v2.

**Anchors.** Before any v3 prediction was scored, an anchors-only run recomputed test truth in the six
already-opened contexts and reproduced the published per-compound RMSE of zero change and of the frozen v1
Ridge and GNN to at most 2.2×10⁻¹⁶ ([truth_anchors.tsv](../reports/v3/truth_anchors.tsv)).

## OP3 — v4 confirmatory

**Source.** [GSE279945](https://www.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/geo/query/acc.cgi?acc=GSE279945): single-cell RNA-seq
of 146 small molecules in peripheral blood mononuclear cells from three donors at 24 h. Raw UMI counts
cover **298,087 cells and 21,265 genes**. The file size is pinned; its SHA256 is recorded by the extraction
kernel at download and compared with an independent local download, and every later kernel chains the
hashes of the extraction outputs. The data were also analysed by others in the NeurIPS 2023 competition.

**What this project read before committing the protocol:** the design metadata and the cell-type counts of
the DMSO wells only, recorded in [op3_pre_protocol_reads.md](../receipts/v4/op3_pre_protocol_reads.md). No
OP3 expression value informed any choice.

**Layout.** Six plates, one donor per plate and two plates per donor. Each plate row is one library holding
exactly one DMSO well. Each of the 141 compounds is dosed at 1 µM, one well per donor. The positive
controls are excluded: belinostat because its dose (0.1 µM) and replicate structure differ, dabrafenib
because of its 48 wells.

**Contexts.** The authors' four reannotated cell types — T, NK, B and myeloid. A measurement unit is the
cells of one type in one well, and its strict matched vehicle is the same cell type in the DMSO well of the
same plate row, sharing library and donor.

**Split.** All 144 standardized structures pass v1's rule; whole scaffold groups were split with seed 42
into 60/15/25, giving **85 training, 21 validation and 35 test** compounds
([manifest](../manifests/v4/op3_compounds.tsv)).

**Eligibility and gates.** A (compound, context) pair is eligible when all three donors have at least 30
treated cells and at least 30 cells in the matched vehicle unit. A context is confirmatory with at least 20
eligible test compounds in at least 10 scaffold groups; below that it is descriptive. In DMSO wells the
median unit sizes are 339 T, 104 myeloid, 91 B and 40.5 NK cells, so NK eligibility was expected to be the
tightest — and the three gate-passing contexts are T, B and myeloid.

**Truth and gene space.** Per donor, the truth is `log1p` CPM of the treated unit minus the matched vehicle
unit over the all-gene library; the three donors are averaged with equal weight. Test truth is first
computed by the evaluation kernel, after every prediction is sealed. The 2,000 genes are the
highest-variance training-compound deltas (v1's rule), restricted to genes detected in at least 5% of DMSO
cells; only training compounds and vehicle cells inform the choice.

## Tahoe-100M — v5 and v6 confirmatory

**Source.** [Tahoe-100M](https://huggingface.co/datasets/tahoebio/Tahoe-100M), Hugging Face revision
`349e2a55…`, CC0. It pools 50 cancer cell lines in every well and holds 379 drugs at 0.05, 0.5 and 5 µM for
24 h. Every file read was hash-checked. The route was streamed Hugging Face parquet in Kaggle CPU kernels
(~429 GB) rather than the Requester-Pays GCS copy.

**What was never read before the protocols were committed:** the published pseudobulk DE files, the
expression-summary columns of `sample_metadata`, and any compound-sample cell count. Design columns only.

**Compounds, shared by both versions.** 336 standardized compounds in 290 scaffold groups, split
**202 / 50 / 84**, the test set covering 77 scaffold groups. v6 reuses this split **byte-for-byte** and
draws no new compounds. Compounds that also appear in sci-Plex3 or OP3 were kept and the overlap reported
(decision D7), because they had never been scored in Tahoe.

**Contexts.** A context is a (cell line, dose).

| | v5 | v6 |
|---|---|---|
| Lines | 10, drawn at random (seed 42) from lines whose median vehicle unit has ≥ 200 cells | 10 others, drawn from the remaining 38-line pool with `default_rng(6)` |
| Named | SNU-1, A-172, HEC-1-A, MIA PaCa-2, C32, H4, LOX-IMVI, NCI-H2347, SW 1088, SW1417 | from the committed v5 manifests, with no new expression read ([design check](../receipts/v6/e1/v6_design_check.json)) |
| Contexts | 30, all passing the count gate, 77–84 eligible test compounds each | 27 of 30 pass; all three failures are CVCL_1577, whose halves leave no responder |
| Test units | 2,464 test pairs | 2,417 eligible keys, of which 1,323 are responders |

**Vehicle and plates.** The strict vehicle is all DMSO_TF cells of the same line on the same plate. Because
about 90 compound wells share one vehicle unit, the protocol adds a plate-anchored training mean (B1p) and
a leave-one-plate-out sensitivity analysis — and B1p went on to be the strongest comparator in both
versions. The replicate unit is the well, usually one per (compound, dose); compounds present on both plate
6 and plate 14 are averaged over the two plates, each against its own plate's vehicle. There are no donors,
so the study cannot separate biological from technical variance except for those duplicated compounds.

**Model cells.** At most 32 cells per well and line train the models; truth and the baselines use all cells.

**v6's measurement split.** This is the structural addition. Each unit's cells, and those of its matched
vehicle unit, are partitioned into two disjoint halves (seed 606; median 678 selection and 679 evaluation
cells per unit). The selection half chooses each key's top-50 differentially expressed genes and decides
whether the key responded; the evaluation half is the truth the endpoint scores against. No measurement
both picks a key's genes and grades it. Scale: 3,388 shards, 11,760 units, 17,698,679 pass-filter cells,
each found exactly once.

**v6's responder gate** (amendment A1) is the standardized root-mean-square of delta over its standard
error, thresholded at 2.772 — the 95th percentile of 140 between-well vehicle statistics. 4,946 of 9,700
keys are responders, and the responder population is the primary one.

**The price of splitting.** Halving the cells behind both the gene choice and the truth makes both noisier
than an unsplit analysis would be. That is the cost of an honest gene choice, and v6's report states it as
a limitation rather than a footnote.

## What these datasets do not support

- **Three datasets are not a field.** Each version's conclusion is about its own compounds, contexts and
  endpoint.
- **Every design here measures training compounds on the same plate as the test compounds,** so a
  plate-anchored mean is always available as a comparator. A genuinely prospective setting — a new plate or
  batch, with no training compound measured alongside — is not represented by any of them, and the
  [synthesis](../reports/synthesis.md) names this as the most interesting thing the six versions never
  tested.
- **No dataset here establishes** efficacy, toxicity, target engagement or clinical benefit.
- **Dose–response shape, time course and single-cell heterogeneity** were never endpoints: v1–v5 scored
  magnitude and direction over 2,000 genes, and v6 scored direction on each key's own top-50.
